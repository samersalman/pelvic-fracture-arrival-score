{{ app_name }}

A bedside point score for adults with a pelvic ring fracture, built from information available in the first minutes after arrival. It sorts patients into a rule-out, observe or escalate stratum for major hemorrhage.

Research use only. The score has internal and temporal validation only and has not been externally validated. It is not a diagnostic device and does not replace clinical judgment.
